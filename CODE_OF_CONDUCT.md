# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work.
Harassment of any kind is not accepted.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the repository's **Security** tab
("Report a vulnerability") and mark it as a conduct report. Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
